# NOCTIS — LEDGER

One row per session. Created in session 19; sessions 1–18 predate it and are not
reconstructed, because a cost invented after the fact is worse than a gap.

**What the columns mean.**

- **wall** — first tool call to last. An hour waiting for a quiet machine costs
  no tokens and a great deal of clock; recording both is the point.
- **in / cache read / out** — as `/cost` reports, in millions to two decimals.
  Cache reads have their own column because they are the only number the
  operator can influence, and they are priced at a tenth of fresh input.
- **USD** — **computed, not copied**: `in × $5 + cache read × $0.50 + out × $25`.
  Where `/cost` reports something different, both are recorded and the row says
  which is which.
- **SEK** — with the rate, in a footnote.
- **kind** — one of `build`, `diagnose`, `measure`, `content`, `tooling`, `mixed`.
- **bought** — one line, plain, what the session produced. Not what it attempted.

**Session 19's cost cells are empty, and the reason is not an omission.** `/cost`
is an interactive CLI command and this session ran non-interactively, so no token
figure was available to record. Per the rule this ledger was created with — *a
gap with a reason beats a guess* — the cells stay empty rather than being
estimated. The wall figure is real: 22:20 to 00:00 local, measured from the first
tool call to the last.

**Machine conditions for session 19, because they bound what its numbers mean.**
`load1` ran 3.4–4.2 throughout against the CONTRACT §0.2 quiet bar of **1.6**,
with the Claude app resident and two orphan vite servers from earlier sessions
that the environment refused to let the session kill. Seven of eight gates are
green; `perfcheck`'s six timing assertions are red and **none of them is a
verdict** at that load. The counts in the same run — draws, triangles, instances,
froxel occupancy — are integers and do not drift, so they are evidence.
